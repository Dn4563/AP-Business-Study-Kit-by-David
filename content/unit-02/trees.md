# Trees

```
THE 4 Ps
Marketing mix
├─ Product: features, quality, branding, life cycle
├─ Price: strategy, markup/margin, elasticity, legal limits
├─ Place: channels (direct/indirect, B2C/B2B), legal limits
└─ Promotion: the five tools, sales funnel, digital metrics
```

```
MARKET RESEARCH
Market research
├─ Primary (you collect it)
│  ├─ Quantitative: surveys, experiments, A/B tests
│  └─ Qualitative: focus groups, interviews
│     (observation can be either)
└─ Secondary (someone else collected it)
   └─ Government reports, census, industry studies, databases
```

```
FACTORS THAT INFLUENCE BUYING
Buying decision
├─ Personal: age, income, education, occupation, lifestyle
├─ Psychological: beliefs, values, motivations, perceptions, past experiences
├─ Social and cultural: family, friends, culture, celebrities, social media
└─ Situational: time, location, mood, store layout, availability
```

```
PROMOTIONAL MIX
Promotion
├─ Media advertising (mass, awareness)
├─ Personal selling (consequential purchases)
├─ Sales promotion (short-term deals)
├─ Direct marketing (targeted, trackable)
└─ Public relations (goodwill, indirect)
```
